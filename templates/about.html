ABOUT TRINITY UNIVERSITY

Training, positioning, and empowering real estate wealth creators.

The institution exists to bridge the gap between theoretical knowledge and practical execution in the African real estate ecosystem.

Our story

From an inaugural class of 22 to 500+ alumni across four continents.

Trinity Real Estate University (TREU) was established in January 2025 with a clear mandate: to train, position, and empower individuals with advanced real estate knowledge, strategic insight, and wealth-building capabilities.

TREU was founded on the conviction that structured education, ethical practice, and practical exposure are the pillars of sustainable real estate success.

Since inception, TREU has focused on developing competent real estate professionals, ethical brokers, and visionary developers equipped to operate at global standards.

Mission

Trinity Real Estate University (TREU) empowers wealth creators through structured real estate education. Our 10-year vision is to raise 10,000 millionaires, develop 500 certified real estate developers, and train 5,000 professionals annually—turning knowledge into strategic opportunity and lasting wealth.

Vision

To become Africa’s leading real estate university within 10 years and a globally recognized real estate institution within 20 years — producing five million trained and empowered real estate professionals and developers worldwide.

Objectives

Our objective is to become Africa’s leading real estate university, achieve global recognition, build a network of 5 million realtors and developers, and shape the future of real estate education and wealth creation.
